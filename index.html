Company overview

BTC Industries Private Limited is a private limited company based in Siwan, Bihar. It has been set up to build and run a cigarette manufacturing facility in the state, with suitable machinery, trained staff and written operating procedures.

Tobacco manufacturing in India is closely regulated — manufacturing permissions, excise and GST, packaging and health-warning rules, workplace safety and pollution control all apply. Our plan is to put these in place first and to begin commercial operations only after the project is found technically and financially feasible.

The project will need investment in premises, machinery, utilities, safety systems and staff training. These costs are being estimated as part of the current planning work.

The project

What we are building

A cigarette manufacturing unit with industrial machinery, ventilation, electrical systems, fire protection and separate areas for each stage of work.

How we will run it

Written procedures, regular machine maintenance, production records, safety training for every worker, and tracking of power use and costs.

What comes first

Feasibility, budget and a phased purchase plan — followed by every required licence — before any major money is spent on the facility.

Siwan,
Bihar

The proposed unit is planned in Siwan district, north-west Bihar. The exact site will be published here once finalised.

Expected to create local employment and technical training opportunities in the district.

Compliance & responsibility

  • All manufacturing licences and statutory approvals obtained before production.
  • Full compliance with COTPA, 2003 — no advertising or promotion of tobacco products.
  • Statutory health warnings on all packaging as prescribed by law.
  • Trade only through licensed distributors, wholesalers and retailers.
  • No sale to persons below 18 years of age.
  • Workplace safety, fire safety and pollution-control norms followed.
  • Regular internal compliance reviews and proper statutory records.

Beware of fraud

BTC Industries is in the planning stage. We are not appointing dealers or distributors at present and do not charge any fee for jobs, interviews or dealerships.
